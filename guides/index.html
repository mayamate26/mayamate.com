Guides

How to check, and fix, whether AI assistants recommend your business. Built on Maya's own measured answers.

  • How to check if ChatGPT recommends your business (and what to do if it doesn't) — Short answer: ask ChatGPT the questions your buyers ask, in a fresh chat, eight different ways, and count how many answers name you. One answer tells you nothing, because answers change from run to run. Eight to sixteen answers give you a number you can track. It takes 15 minutes and costs nothing. Or use the free check if your category is already measured.
  • Do ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini agree on who to recommend? — Short answer: they don't agree on #1, but they do agree on a shortlist. I asked four AI setups the same buying questions in 14 categories. In none of the 14 did all four name the same company first. In every one of the 14, though, there was a group of 2 to 5 companies that all four named somewhere. If you're trying to get recommended by AI, that shortlist is the thing to aim for. "We rank #1 in ChatGPT" is mostly noise.
